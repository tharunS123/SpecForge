Build a web app for async daily standups for small teams. People sign in with Google or an email magic link. Every weekday each team member answers three questions: what they did yesterday, what they're doing today, and any blockers. The team lead sees everyone's answers for a day on one page, and gets a Slack message whenever someone posts a blocker. People can only see their own team's standups. Keep 90 days of history, then delete older entries.
